Key facts about timing, cost, and terms

Use this checklist when reviewing a loan agency or a particular offer:

  • What it covers: Ask whether the agency can compare personal loans, refinancing, business financing, or only certain types of borrowing.
  • Typical timing: The process varies with the information requested and each lender’s review. Ask what happens after you submit details and when you may hear about next steps.
  • Cost: The cost depends on the lender’s offer and your circumstances. Review the interest rate, any applicable fees, repayment schedule, and total amount you would repay.
  • How to compare: Look at the same details across offers, including payment amounts, loan length, fees, and any conditions. Check who the lender is and read the agreement before accepting.

You may hear a few common terms. Principal is the amount borrowed before interest and fees. Interest rate is the rate charged for borrowing, while loan term is the period for repayment. A secured loan is connected to collateral; ask what property or asset, if any, is involved in an offer.
